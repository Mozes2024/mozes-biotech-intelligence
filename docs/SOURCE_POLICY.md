# Source Policy

## Discovery sources

ClinicalTrials.gov is used to discover trial windows, trial structure and sponsor information. A Primary Completion Date is not treated as a readout date.

## Verification sources

An actionable catalyst date should be supported by at least one primary source:

- FDA or Federal Register;
- SEC filing/exhibit;
- company investor relations / company press release.

Conference listings and peer-reviewed papers can support context but are not a substitute for sponsor/FDA confirmation of a future corporate readout date.

Secondary calendars and news are leads only. A secondary-only date is quarantined.

## Provenance

Every promoted event stores the source URL, publication date, retrieved time and the extracted statement used to support the event.

## Base-rate context

BIO/Informa/QLS 2011-2020 reports an overall Phase III to NDA/BLA transition rate of 57.8% and an NDA/BLA to approval transition rate of 90.6%. Oncology Phase III to NDA/BLA was 47.7%, versus 61.3% for non-oncology.

These are program transition rates, not literal probabilities that an individual upcoming topline is positive. v0.2 therefore uses them only as contextual priors and labels its evidence scores as uncalibrated.
